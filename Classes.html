Got Questions?

Class FAQs for Beginners

What should I bring to my first class?

Bring a bottle of water, a small sweat towel, and wear comfortable athletic clothes with supportive training shoes. We provide all equipment and hydration stations.

Do I need to be in good shape before joining?

Not at all! Every single exercise in our classes can be scaled up or down by our coaches. Whether it is your day 1 or year 5, you will get a workout tailored to you.

How early should I arrive before class starts?

Please arrive 10 to 15 minutes prior to your first class. This gives our coaches time to give you a tour, discuss any prior injuries, and help you get set up comfortably.

How do I book my trial class?

Click any "Book a Trial Class" button on this page, fill out your quick details, and select your preferred time slot!
